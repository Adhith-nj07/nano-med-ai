REPORT GENERATED: 2026-10-02 | TARGET PARTICLE: PLGA-PEG Doxorubicin (NP-AG-04)

1. EXECUTIVE FORMULATION SUMMARY

Nanoparticle formulation NP-AG-04 consists of a PLGA (50:50, MW ~45 kDa) core encapsulated with Doxorubicin HCl (8.5% w/w loading) and surface-functionalized with PEG 2000 Da and Folate ligands. Hydrodynamic diameter is 68.5 nm with a PDI of 0.12.

2. MACHINE LEARNING PREDICTION METRICS

Zeta Potential is predicted at −24.8 mV (± 2.1 mV, 95% CI), placing the formulation in the electrostatically stable domain. Cytotoxicity endpoint prediction on A549 cell lines indicates a Nontoxic profile (14% toxicity risk probability) under standard 24h exposure at 50 µg/mL.

3. BIOPHYSICAL KINETICS & MODEL LIMITATIONS

Korsmeyer-Peppas in-vitro release kinetics modeling indicates Fickian spherical matrix diffusion (n = 0.45). Model valid region covers up to 48.0h (45.8% cumulative release). Trajectories beyond 48.0h represent mathematical extrapolation and require experimental validation.

RECOMMENDATION: NP-AG-04 is recommended for experimental shortlist inclusion. In-vitro cell uptake and dissolution testing in USP Apparatus 4 are required before in-vivo translational phase.
